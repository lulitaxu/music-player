What it does

01

Two catalogs, one library

Search and play YouTube Music and Audius side by side, from one search box and one library.

02

Downloads that stay offline

Save tracks, albums and playlists into the offline vault and play them anywhere, no connection needed.

03

Lyrics that keep time

Synced lines follow the song as it plays, lit by the artwork’s own colors, with the current line glowing.

04

A real background player

Lock-screen and notification controls, gapless transitions, crossfade up to twelve seconds, and a sleep timer for the night.

05

Tuned to your taste

Liked songs, listening stats, history and made-for-you mixes, all built from your own plays.
